10-minute routine

Train consistency, not magic numbers

  1. 2 min: warm up crosshair placement at head height.
  2. 3 min: practice short upward drags with your preferred close-range weapon.
  3. 3 min: alternate stationary and moving targets.
  4. 2 min: record hit consistency and adjust one sensitivity value by 3–5 points.

Avoid macros, scripts, or tools that automate firing or modify the game client; they may violate game rules and risk your account.
